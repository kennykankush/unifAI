## Limits

The team token can restrict which projects are visible.

Usage covers the UTC calendar month, not necessarily the billing cycle. No automatic invoice history, historical upgrade backfill, renewal inference, credit reconciliation, runtime logs or Vercel-specific spike alerts were added. Gross costs never establish cash paid.

Native QA used synthetic data at the existing fixed wallet-sheet size; VoiceOver, other display sizes and actual token entry were not exercised.
